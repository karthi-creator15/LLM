| Family | Owner | Open/closed weights | Context window | Typical strengths |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GPT | OpenAI | Closed for hosted GPT models | Varies by model; verify the selected model's official model page | General reasoning, coding, multimodal capabilities, tool use |
| Claude | Anthropic | Closed | Varies by model; verify the selected model's official model page | Long-document work, writing, coding, analysis |
